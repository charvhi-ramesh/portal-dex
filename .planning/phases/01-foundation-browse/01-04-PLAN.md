Why every usePathname consumer sits inside Suspense: with Cache Components, usePathname suspends on routes whose dynamic params are not known at build time (Phase 3 adds `/character/[id]`), and an unwrapped consumer in the root layout would fail that build (node_modules/next/dist/docs/01-app/03-api-reference/04-functions/use-pathname.md). Phase 1 routes are static, so the fallbacks never show in Phase 1.
