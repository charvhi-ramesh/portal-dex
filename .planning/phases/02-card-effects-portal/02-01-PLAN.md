## Flagged Assumptions

- **[edge CARD-03 / unclassified, unresolved]** Assumed edges, not probe-verified: a double click flips twice and ends on the front; Enter and Space use native button activation (no custom key handler); flip state is local to each card, keyed by character id, so flipping one card never flips another and a newly revealed portal card starts face up; the star and link are siblings above the flip button, so their clicks never reach it.
- **[edge CARD-04 / unclassified, unresolved]** Assumed edges, not probe-verified: any status other than Alive or Dead uses the unknown frame (existing `statusKey`); frames, glow and shimmer are drawn with borders and pseudo-elements that never change the card's box size; Dead desaturation applies to the avatar photo only, not to the initial fallback tile.
- **D-01 adjustment (costly, flagged):** the frame border and clipping move from the `article` onto each face, because a backface-hidden 3D flip cannot show the right face when the rotating content is clipped by an ancestor with overflow hidden. DOM order and the sibling relationships of D-01 are unchanged, and the outer size is unchanged, so `CardSkeleton` still matches.
- **Ring technique:** the focus ring is a pseudo-element on the article (teal 2 px border with a 2 px navy inset band), not an `outline`, because the Alive glow would otherwise paint over the navy gap (SPEC X5 "navy inner ring plus teal outer ring with offset").
- **SPEC section 6 hover glow:** a status-coloured outer glow on hover and focus is added here with opacity only; its intensity is tuned in Phase 4.
- **Canon referral (not minted):** rendering API strings as HTML is canon injection, covered by `/gsd-secure-phase` and T-02-01 below.
- **Open review warnings WR-02, WR-03, WR-06** (Browse focus handling) are untouched: this plan does not modify `components/browse/browse-view.tsx`, and the article keeps `tabIndex={-1}` so its focus hand-off still works.
