## Flagged Assumptions

- **[edge CARD-05 / unclassified, unresolved]** Assumed edges, not probe-verified: "toward the cursor" means the card face turns to face the pointer (rotateY = (nx - 0.5) x 2 x 12, rotateX = (0.5 - ny) x 2 x 12, with nx and ny the pointer position as 0 to 1 fractions of the card), pinned by unit tests and confirmed visually; only `pointerType === "mouse"` tilts; the card rect is measured on pointer enter and reused during the move, so no layout read happens per move; a card that is flipped still tilts; turning reduced motion on while hovering snaps the card flat.
- **Sheen technique (discretion):** the sheen is a radial-gradient spot moved with Motion `x` and `y` transforms inside a clipped, pointer-events-none overlay, rather than a moving `background-position`, so only transform and opacity animate (SPEC section 6).
- **Spring values (discretion):** stiffness 300 and damping 30 give a response of roughly 150 to 200 ms (SPEC section 6 hover and tilt timing); Phase 4 may tune them.
